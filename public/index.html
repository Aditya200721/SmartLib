ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Modernizing Institutional Library Operations

Smart Lib is a comprehensive library management system designed to simplify and modernize library operations. It enables administrators and librarians to manage books, members, borrowing records, returns, and fines through a centralized platform.

Real-time data and analytics help institutions understand borrowing patterns, improve resource utilization, and provide students with better access to knowledge.
